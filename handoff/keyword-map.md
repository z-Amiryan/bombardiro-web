## Don't target

| Topic                                          | Why                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Children's parties ("games for 7 year olds")   | They want physical games, and this is not a children's app  |
| Drinking and betting                           | Against the app's 4+ rating policy                          |
| Play online ("алиас онлайн", "alias game online") | There's no browser game. Don't promise one               |
| FIFA, UEFA, "World Cup 2026"                   | Trademarks                                                  |
| The Bombardiro Crocodilo meme                  | Unrelated                                                   |
| Pages per player or club                       | Rights risk, and those searchers want stats                 |
| Solo quiz searches ("угадай футболиста")       | Wrong intent                                                |

Don't generate pages from the word list. Google treats that as scaled content abuse.

## Naming

- Always pair "Bombardiro" with football in titles and descriptions. On its own, the word currently autocompletes
  to an unrelated meme.
- Write "football (soccer)" where English readers may be American.
