# Copy rules

Every statement about the app must be true of the app as it ships.

## Facts you can use

- 2–4 teams share one phone. Pass and play, one device, one room.
- Turn time 30, 60, 90 or 120 seconds. 5–8 words per card. Target 51, 101, 151 or 201.
- Six categories: Players, Clubs, Tactics & Trophies, Stadiums, Legends, World Cup.
- Nearly 1,700 words per language. Easy and Hard.
- Clearing a card brings a new one on the same clock. Only the timer ends a turn, or tapping END.
- Equal turns once a team reaches the target. A tie at the top plays another round.
- Only the latest turn can be corrected.
- Works offline. No account. English, Russian and Armenian. Dark and light themes.
- New and corrected words arrive without an app update.
- The words are translated by people, not machines (confirmed by the owner).
- Free. iPhone and iPad.

## Never say

- Add your own words, custom decks, or edit any round.
- Swipe (words are tapped), or team colours (teams get crests).
- Online multiplayer, or play remotely.
- Thousands of words, or 10,000+.
- For kids. The app is rated 4+, which doesn't make it a children's app.
- Ad-free forever. "No ads" appears only in the two FAQ answers.
- Official, licensed, endorsed, or any partnership.
- FIFA, UEFA, or "World Cup 2026". "World Cup" is only the name of a category.
- Best, #1, ultimate, or any other unprovable superlative.
- Coming soon, launching soon, or waitlist.
- Anything about a browser version, because there isn't one.

## Voice

- **English:** short and kinetic, with matchday words (kick off, full time, whistle, deep cuts, top scorer).
  Second person.
- **Russian:** «ты» and imperatives. Real football speech (бомбардир, свисток, знатоки, доигрывается). No gendered
  past tense for the reader. «Ёлочки», em dashes with spaces, and ё. People are «описывай», words are «объясняй».
  Don't mention Instagram on Russian pages.
- **Armenian:** «դու» imperatives and short sentences. ։ and ՝ for punctuation, and the question mark on the
  stressed vowel. Use the app's terms: տարբերանշան, խաղացողներ, Հեշտ, Դժվար.

Use the app's terms exactly. Check `content/copy/` first.
