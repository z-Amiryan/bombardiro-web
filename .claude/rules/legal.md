# Legal and platform rules

- **App Store badge:** only the files in `assets/badges/`. One per page, at least 40 px high, clear space of a
  quarter of its height. Never modify, angle or animate it, and keep it below the main message.
- **App Store wording:** "Available on the App Store". Never "at the App Store", "Apple App Store" or
  "downloadable". "App Store" stays in English in every language.
- **Credit line:** `footer.apple` once per page, in the footer.
- **Apple devices:** official bezels only, used as they are, or no device at all. No tilting, cropping,
  animating, flipping, shadows, reflections or overlaps. Copy goes beside the device, not on it.
- **Football names:** players and clubs as plain text only. Never a photo, crest, kit, logo or league mark.
- **Independence:** `footer.independent` appears on every page.
- **Age rating:** the app is 4+. No alcohol or betting brands, no drinking games, no gambling, no adult content.
- **Privacy:** no cookies of our own, no forms, no third-party requests. If this changes, the privacy page must
  change in the same PR.
