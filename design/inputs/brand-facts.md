# Bombardiro brand facts

These describe the app as it exists today, taken from its code. They are facts, not a brief for the website.

## Name

- **Bombardiro.** It plays on «бомбардир», the Russian football word for a prolific goalscorer.
- The in-app tagline is "The football word game". In Russian and Armenian the game is also called the football
  Alias: «футбольный Алиас», «ֆուտբոլային Ալիաս».

## The app's visual system

The app's dark theme is called "Slate night" and its light theme "Chalk grass". The default follows the phone,
then dark.

## The app's type

| Use                 | English             | Russian             | Armenian                     |
| ------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Display             | Archivo 900 italic  | Noto Sans 900 italic | Noto Sans Armenian 900, upright |
| Interface           | Space Grotesk       | Noto Sans           | Noto Sans Armenian           |

- Armenian has no italic cut in these families.
- The BOMBARDIRO wordmark stays in Archivo in every language. "BOMBAR" is in the text colour and "DIRO" in the
  accent colour.
