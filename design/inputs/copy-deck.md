## How to play

### English

- **meta title:** How to play Bombardiro: football Alias rules
- **meta description:** The rules in a minute: pick categories, line up 2–4 teams, describe the words without saying them and beat the whistle. Settings and fair-play rules explained.
- **h1:** How to play
- **lead:** Describe the word. Don't say it. Score the goal.
- **stepsTitle:** The rules
- **steps 1 title:** Pick your categories
- **steps 1 text:** Choose the categories you want in the match: players, clubs, World Cup and more.
- **steps 2 title:** Line up teams
- **steps 2 text:** Add 2–4 teams, name them and pick a crest. Then pass the phone around the table.
- **steps 3 title:** Describe, don't say it
- **steps 3 text:** The active player describes the words on the card. Saying the word itself is a foul: leave it unmarked and move on to the next.
- **steps 4 title:** Tap a word, score a goal
- **steps 4 text:** When your team guesses it, tap the word to bank a goal. Tap again to undo a mistake.
- **steps 5 title:** Beat the clock
- **steps 5 text:** Score as many as you can before the whistle. The clock ends the turn, or tap END to finish it early.
- **steps 6 title:** First to the target wins
- **steps 6 text:** Hand off and repeat. Once a team reaches the target, the round is played out so everyone gets equal turns, and the top score lifts the trophy.
- **settings title:** Your match, your rules
- **settings items 1 label:** Turn time
- **settings items 1 value:** 30, 60, 90 or 120 seconds
- **settings items 1 text:** How many seconds one player gets to describe words. When the time runs out, the turn passes to the next team.
- **settings items 2 label:** Words per card
- **settings items 2 value:** 5 to 8
- **settings items 2 text:** How many words are dealt on one card. Clear them all and a fresh card arrives on the same clock: the timer ends the turn, not the card.
- **settings items 3 label:** Points to win
- **settings items 3 value:** 51, 101, 151 or 201
- **settings items 3 text:** The target score for the match. Once a team reaches it, the round is played out so every team gets the same number of turns, then the highest score wins. A tie at the top plays another round.
- **settings items 4 label:** Number of teams
- **settings items 4 value:** 2 to 4
- **settings items 4 text:** How many teams the match starts with. You can rename them, pick crests, and add or remove teams before kick-off.
- **settings items 5 label:** Difficulty
- **settings items 5 value:** Easy or Hard
- **settings items 5 text:** Which word pool the cards come from. Easy uses the best-known words in your categories, Hard the more obscure ones.
- **fair title:** Fair to the last kick
- **fair items 1:** No word repeats in a match until your chosen categories run out.
- **fair items 2:** Once a team reaches the target, the round is played out so everyone gets the same number of turns.
- **fair items 3:** Level at the top? An extra round decides it.
- **fair items 4:** Missed a tap as the whistle blew? Tap the latest score on the scoreboard and switch the word between GOAL and MISS.
- **tips title:** How to describe well
- **tips items 1:** Players: try the club, the position, the country or a famous moment.
- **tips items 2:** Clubs: try the city, the nickname, the colours or the biggest rival.
- **tips items 3:** Stadiums: try the city, the home team or a match everyone remembers.
- **tips items 4:** Stuck? Leave the word unmarked and move on. The clock won't wait.
- **video title:** Watch a match
- **video caption:** A 25-second look at the app, from picking categories to full time.
