5 · Motion

Easing tokens: --ease-out: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) for most UI · --ease-in: cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1) for exits · --ease-spring: cubic-bezier(.3,1.3,.5,1) for flips only. Nothing is animated before first paint, and every H1 is static text.
